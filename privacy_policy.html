Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 9, 2026 | Last Updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cyan International ("Company", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects your information when you use our mobile applications and digital services available via the Google Play Store.

No Data Selling

Cyan International does not sell your personal or sensitive data to third parties.

Encrypted Data

All data transmitted between your device and servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

Data Deletion

You have the right to request full account and personal data erasure at any time.

1. Overview

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through compliance with this policy. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully to understand our policies and practices regarding your information and how we will treat it. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, your choice is not to use our applications.

2. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information from and about users of our applications, including:

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, email address (e.g., when registering an account), phone number, and unique device identifiers.
  • Account & Authentication Credentials: Encrypted passwords, security tokens, and authentication session data.
  • Usage Data: Application launch frequency, feature interaction, crash logs, diagnostic performance reports, and system log files.
  • Financial Information: Payment details processed securely through compliant third-party payment gateways (e.g., BillDesk, Stripe). We do not directly store credit card numbers or banking PINs.

3. Android Device Permissions

To provide specific functional features, our applications may request access to certain system permissions on your Android device:

Permission Purpose / Usage Optional / Required
INTERNET Allows the app to connect to network services and server endpoints. Required
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Checks active internet connectivity status to prevent failed network requests. Required
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Delivers account updates, system alerts, and security notifications. Optional
CAMERA / GALLERY Allows uploading profile images, document scans, or attachments within the app. Optional

4. How We Use Your Information

Cyan International uses the collected information for legitimate operational and business purposes, including:

  • Providing, maintaining, and improving our application services and backend APIs.
  • Authenticating user sessions and securing user accounts against unauthorized access.
  • Processing financial transactions and sending receipt notifications.
  • Monitoring app stability, debugging runtime crashes, and optimizing user interface responsiveness.
  • Complying with legal obligations, enforce terms of service, and prevent fraud.

5. Third-Party Services & Sub-Processors

We may share limited anonymized data with trusted third-party service providers to support application operation and performance monitoring. These services operate under strict confidentiality agreements:

  • Google Play Services: Used for core application distribution, updates, and licensing.
  • Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics: Used for collecting anonymous crash logs and stability monitoring.
  • Payment Gateways: Used for secure transaction processing.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion Policy

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

How to Request Data Deletion:

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policy rules, users can request the complete deletion of their account and associated data. To request account erasure, email us at cloud.cyaninternational@gmail.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request. We will process and confirm deletion within 30 calendar days.

7. Children's Privacy

Our applications are not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn we have collected or received personal information from a child under 13 without verification of parental consent, we will delete that information immediately.

8. Data Security Measures

We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. All network communications are encrypted using HTTPS (SSL/TLS), and sensitive database storage uses industry-standard encryption protocols.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your region, you have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, port, or restrict processing of your information. You may exercise these rights at any time by contacting our privacy compliance team.
